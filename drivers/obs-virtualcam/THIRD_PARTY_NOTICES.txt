OBS Virtual Camera driver (obs-virtualcam-module32.dll, obs-virtualcam-module64.dll)
====================================================================================

These two DLLs are the DirectShow "OBS Virtual Camera" filter, taken
unmodified from the OBS Studio project (https://obsproject.com,
https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio), used here strictly as a
virtual camera driver -- MediaFlow does not use, embed, or link against
any other part of OBS Studio.

OBS Studio is licensed under the GNU General Public License v2.0 (GPLv2).
Full license text: https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/blob/master/COPYING
Source code for these files: https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/tree/master/plugins/win-dshow/virtualcam-module

MediaFlow registers these DLLs as a normal DirectShow video source (the
same "regsvr32 /i /s" step OBS Studio's own installer performs) so that
its own producer (src/ObsVirtualCamWriter.*) can feed them directly --
OBS Studio's application/UI is never installed or required.
